10. Learning Reflection

In Database Fundamentals, I learned how to plan and create databases, use SQL commands, and manage records. I practiced creating tables, inserting and updating data, filtering information, and joining tables. Some topics, especially joins and subqueries, were challenging, but running queries step by step helped me understand them better. These activities improved my confidence in working with databases and gave me skills I can use in future IT projects.

11. Course Learning Summary

Overall learning experience and achievements in ITC C201 – Database Fundamentals

Throughout ITC C201 – Database Fundamentals, I gained a better understanding of how databases are planned, designed, created, and managed. During the prelim period, I learned about database concepts, conceptual models, entity relationship diagrams, and normalization. In the midterm period, I gained practical experience using Oracle APEX to create tables, insert records, and modify or update data. For the final term, I practiced SQL SELECT statements, aliases, arithmetic and concatenation operators, WHERE conditions, sorting, and joining tables. Completing the activities, quizzes, and projects helped me connect the concepts discussed in class with actual database tasks. Although some topics and SQL operations required more practice, these experiences improved my problem-solving skills and confidence in working with databases. I will use what I learned as a foundation for future programming and information technology projects.
